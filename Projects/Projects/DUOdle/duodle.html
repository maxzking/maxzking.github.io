DUOdle

How to Play

DUOdle is a spin-off of the game Wordle by powerlanguish.

Guess your target word in 4 tries and before your opponent.

Each player has a different target word they must guess.

After both players have guessed, the color of the tiles change to show how close your guess was.

P is in the word in the correct spot

A is in th eword in the incorrect spot

I, R, and S are not in the word

Cross-scoring: your opponent's guess is scored against your target word, and vice-versa.
